    Payments platform

          MMSegNet

          Multimodal fusion for brain image segmentation

          A CNN that fuses MRI, CT and PET scans before segmenting, because each modality sees something the others miss: MRI shows soft tissue, CT shows bone, and PET shows metabolic activity. Fusing them gives clinicians one clearer view of a lesion.

          • CNNs
          • Image fusion
